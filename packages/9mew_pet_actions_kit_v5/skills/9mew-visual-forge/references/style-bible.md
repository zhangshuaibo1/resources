# 9mew style and character bible

## Selected identity

Use `assets/naimiao-master.png` as the sole visual identity authority for the current main cat. The user selected this first generated image for the website pet. Its source/hash and rejected outputs are in `assets/identity.json`. Scene samples provide composition/material context only. New angle sheets are inferred design aids, not replacement identity masters. This selection authorizes the reference direction; it does not declare every derivative or 3D interpretation approved.

Preserve these recognizable traits for 奶喵 / the official 喵灵 appearance:

- Fine fluffy cream-white fur with warm ivory shadows, directional cheek/chest strands and soft edge wisps. Fur contributes to the silhouette; a smooth surface with a normal map alone does not reproduce it.
- A broad rounded face, full cheeks and soft compact chin. For a same-pose comparison, preserve the reference head tilt, with the viewer-right eye lower than the viewer-left. A requested head turn or body action may change this orientation while preserving facial proportions. Avoid a vertically stretched egg-shaped head or pinched cheeks.
- Large gray-brown eyes seated inside shaped upper/lower eyelids, with softly almond-rounded openings, readable iris/pupil and restrained reflections. Preserve placement, spacing, eye-corner shape and expression. Avoid protruding circular glass balls, concentric rim rings or blank black beads; merely shrinking the eyes does not fix their construction.
- Soft triangular ears with broad roots, outward flare, blush inner recesses, curved rims and fine edge fur. Match their relationship to the tilted head; avoid long upright triangle plates.
- A tiny dusty-pink nose above two soft muzzle pads, a short philtrum, delicate mouth and rounded chin. Preserve cheek-to-muzzle transitions; avoid a flat face with an attached nose and drawn smile.
- A compact, softly full sitting body, natural shoulder/chest/haunch volumes and short feline limbs. Resolve how the supporting forepaw and hind paws bear weight; avoid a long straight torso and cylindrical legs.
- Rounded paws with curved toes enclosing the pink pads, a softly bent wrist on the raised forepaw and visible toe divisions on grounded feet. Pads belong within the paw shape; avoid a flat palm with separate circular discs.
- A full, fluffy tail curling upward from its low body/ground relationship, with deliberate thickness taper, clear attachment and a softly narrowing tip. Preserve the plume-like silhouette and curl placement; avoid a thin smooth ring-shaped tube with a blunt end.
- A small sakura silk scarf and a tiny warm-gold four-point star clasp. Treat them as identity cues, not a costume that hides the cat.
- A welcoming, curious and calm personality. Expression changes must preserve the face.

Maintain real feline structure for ordinary poses. Resolve body weight, shoulder/hip position, elbow/hock bends and paw contact before adding effects. Avoid disconnected spheres, human hands, duplicate feet, fused paws, unintended extra tails, chopped ears and joints hidden behind oversized props.

## Art direction

Preserve the selected high-quality 2D illustration: tactile short fur, satin fabric, pearl ceramic, restrained translucent details, generous visual breathing room and beautiful soft lighting. Volumetric shading is an aesthetic property of the image; it does not require a 3D model. Current product delivery uses high-quality 2D art and actual requested character animation, with static art retained as fallback. Use graceful cats with a clear action, not generic mascot blobs surrounded by stock symbols.

Use the PNG's real alpha for transparent assets; do not paint a checkerboard or opaque vignette into a supposed cutout. Inspect transparent edges on both warm ivory and midnight backgrounds.

## Cultivation, mythology and cosmic fantasy

The world is 九喵宇宙: vast star domains, celestial paths, spirit gardens, gentle cultivation, mythic companions and imaginative forms. Use these themes positively rather than reducing the brand to neutral enterprise clip art.

Suitable motifs include star trails, cloud-like mist, floating jade/pearl arcs, soft spirit ribbons, a luminous heart-star, constellations, small talismans without readable text, halo-like orbital structures and quiet spirit gardens. Give each scene one focal action and a coherent physical or explicitly magical relationship.

Natural-cat and fantasy-cat routes coexist:

- **Natural route:** Respect breed/coat distinction and feline anatomy; vary environment, pose, expression and fitting accessories.
- **Fantasy route:** Preserve character identity while allowing an explicitly requested celestial coat gradient, glowing fur tips, mist ribbon, star freckles, spirit wings or deliberate multi-tail silhouette. Describe the change as an intentional branch. Keep count, attachment and silhouette readable.

The four model service tiers are 喵学徒、喵大师、喵真人、喵仙帝. Use them in product labels and explanation. Do not infer a pet's appearance, battle strength or payment permission from these labels.

## Props and compositions

Design props for the cat's scale, paws and action. Pearl arcs, linen pages, small ceramic stepping stones and soft petals work with the selected material language. A lock or shield is not the universal prop for every trust-related page.

For collaboration, create clear reciprocal actions and space between silhouettes. For knowledge, show exploration and connection. For privacy, show protection and comfortable boundaries. For terms, show a shared agreement without pretending a user has consented. For evolution, make an understandable before/after identity relationship.

Wide hero scenes reserve roughly 35–40% calm copy area. Put text, buttons, prices and interface facts in real localized UI. Use a separate mobile layout or stack copy around the complete art rather than cutting off paws/ears with a cover crop.

## Additional breeds and individuals

Create a new `character_id` for a new individual. Keep shared material, lighting and scene language while preserving distinct head shape, ear shape, coat length/pattern and body proportions. Orange tabby and tuxedo describe coat patterns; do not equate every coat with a pedigree breed. The main selected cat has no certified breed claim.

View suitable actual authorized references when a precise breed is important. Use the bundled main cat as a style reference only for new individuals; do not accidentally copy its white coat and face into every character.

## Review anchors

For current 2D work use [likeness-review.md](likeness-review.md): original identity and image quality first, coherent composition second, then the required action performance and delivery optimization. Use the rejected website model only as clearly labeled failure evidence, never as a new reference or shortcut base for preserving identity.

Review six questions on the actual result: Is this recognizably the intended cat even without its scarf/star cues? Is the pose elegant and anatomically coherent? Does the prop fit its action? Do lighting and materials match? Does the requested fantasy change remain readable? Is the intended page still usable and honest?

Use these questions to select and refine. An automated file check cannot answer them or promote an asset to approved.

## Current motion policy

Preserve the selected image as the fallback and never promote a lower-quality redraw merely to increase action count. Current pet work requires real body-part movement: locomotion, jumping, expressions and daily behaviors. Whole-image movement and separate star accents remain decorative motion-posters; they do not fulfill these actions. Read [animation-workflow.md](animation-workflow.md) before making action sheets, timing or a player. Correct unsuccessful sequences and mark their action status honestly; a static fallback does not complete the requested animation. Keep 3D deferred.
